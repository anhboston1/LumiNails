Privacy Policy

Last Updated: June 2026

Lumi Nails respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information when you interact with our salon, website, appointment system, and SMS communications.

Information We Collect

We may collect the following information:

  • Name
  • Phone number
  • Email address
  • Appointment details
  • Service preferences
  • Communication preferences

How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Schedule and manage appointments
  • Send appointment confirmations and reminders
  • Respond to customer inquiries
  • Provide customer support
  • Send promotional offers and updates if you have opted in

SMS Communications

By providing your phone number and opting in, you agree to receive SMS messages from Lumi Nails regarding appointments, reminders, customer service, and promotional offers.

Message frequency may vary. Message and data rates may apply. You may opt out at any time by replying STOP. For assistance, reply HELP or contact us directly.

Information Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing or promotional purposes.

Your mobile information will not be sold or shared with third parties for promotional or marketing purposes.

We may share information only with service providers necessary to operate our business, such as appointment scheduling, payment processing, and communication platforms.

Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your personal information from unauthorized access, disclosure, or misuse.
