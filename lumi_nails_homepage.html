About Lumi Nails

A cleaner, healthier nail experience in Plaistow.

Lumi Nails brings together modern nail care, a relaxing spa atmosphere, and a clean beauty philosophy designed for guests who want to look polished while feeling comfortable and cared for.

We focus on non-toxic, acrylic-free, and natural-feeling services in a calm, fresh, and thoughtfully designed environment. Every detail is meant to support beauty, wellness, and peace of mind.

Whether you are stopping in for a quick refresh or indulging in a longer self-care visit, our goal is simple: help you feel beautiful, confident, and naturally refreshed.

Why Choose Lumi Nails

Luxury nail care with a cleaner approach.

Every visit is designed to feel refined, relaxing, and naturally beautiful.

Clean Beauty Philosophy

We focus on modern nail services that feel lighter, cleaner, and more natural, using thoughtfully selected products in a fresh and comfortable space.

Acrylic-Free Experience

Instead of traditional acrylic systems, we specialize in services that support the look and feel of healthy nails while still delivering beautiful, long-lasting results.

  • Gel polish
  • Dipping powder
  • Gel-X and modern gel systems
  • Organic spa treatments

Cleanliness You Can Trust

Your comfort and safety come first. We follow strict sanitation and hygiene practices to create a visit that feels safe, polished, and worry-free.

Personalized, Relaxing Care

We take time to understand your preferences, nail health, and style so your results feel elevated, intentional, and uniquely yours.
