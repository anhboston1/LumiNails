About Lumi Nails

Welcome to Lumi Nails, where beauty, wellness, and modern nail care come together in a refined spa environment. Located in Plaistow, New Hampshire, Lumi Nails was created to provide a relaxing escape while delivering exceptional nail services with the highest level of care and professionalism.

Our salon specializes in luxurious manicures, spa pedicures, gel polish, dipping powder, and organic nail treatments designed to enhance both beauty and nail health. We carefully select premium, non-toxic products and maintain strict sanitation standards to ensure every service is safe, clean, and comfortable.

At Lumi Nails, we believe nail care should be more than just a routine service—it should be a moment to relax and recharge. Our skilled technicians focus on personalized service, taking the time to understand your style and preferences while delivering precise, beautiful results.

Whether you're visiting for a quick refresh or indulging in a full spa experience, Lumi Nails is dedicated to helping you look polished, feel confident, and leave feeling renewed.

Our mission

At Lumi Nails, our mission is to redefine nail care through clean, non-toxic, and mindful beauty.

We are committed to offering organic, vegan, and acrylic-free nail services in a calm, well-ventilated environment that prioritizes health, safety, and relaxation.

Every detail of the Lumi experience is thoughtfully designed to enhance natural beauty while supporting overall well-being—so every guest can feel confident, cared for, and truly refreshed.

Why Choose Lumi Nails

A Cleaner, Healthier Nail Experience

At Lumi Nails, we believe nail care should enhance your beauty without compromising your health. That’s why we focus on a clean, non-toxic approach to nail services, using carefully selected products that are safer for both our clients and technicians.

Our salon is designed to provide a well-ventilated, fresh, and relaxing environment, so you can enjoy your service comfortably without the strong chemical smells often found in traditional nail salons.

Acrylic-Free Philosophy

Lumi Nails proudly focuses on natural nail care and modern alternatives. Instead of traditional acrylic systems, we specialize in services like:

  • Gel polish

  • Dipping powder

  • Gel-X and modern gel systems

  • Organic spa treatments

These services are designed to protect and strengthen natural nails while still delivering beautiful, long-lasting results.

Cleanliness You Can Trust

Your safety and comfort are our top priorities. We follow strict sanitation and hygiene practices to ensure every visit is safe and worry-free.

Our standards include:

  • Thoroughly sanitized tools and equipment

  • Clean, organized workstations

  • Disposable liners for pedicure services

  • A fresh, comfortable environment for every guest

Relaxing Spa-Inspired Atmosphere

Lumi Nails is more than just a nail salon—it’s a place to pause, relax, and recharge.

From the moment you walk in, our calm and welcoming atmosphere is designed to help you unwind while enjoying high-quality nail care performed with attention to detail.

Personalized Service

We believe every client deserves individualized care. Our technicians take the time to understand your preferences, nail health, and style so we can deliver results that feel uniquely yours.

Whether you prefer a natural, elegant look or something more creative, we’re here to bring your vision to life.

A Modern Nail Salon Experience

Conveniently located in Plaistow, New Hampshire, Lumi Nails proudly serves clients from Plaistow, Haverhill, Atkinson, Hampstead, and surrounding communities.

Our mission is simple: to provide a clean, modern, and relaxing nail salon experience where you can feel confident, refreshed, and beautifully yourself.
