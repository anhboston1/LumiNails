Frequently Asked Questions

Which nail extension is best?

Gel X is great for lightweight length, acrylic for strong dramatic sets, and dip full set for durable color and length.

Can extensions look natural?

Yes. We can customize length, shape, and color for a natural look.

Can I add designs to extensions?

Yes. Extensions are a great base for French, chrome, ombré, and custom nail art.

How long do extensions last?

Most extensions last about 2 to 4 weeks depending on the service and aftercare.

Do you help me choose the right option?

Yes. We can recommend the best service based on your natural nails and desired look.
