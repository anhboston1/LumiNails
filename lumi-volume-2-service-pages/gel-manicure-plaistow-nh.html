Frequently Asked Questions

How long does a Gel Manicure last?

A gel manicure usually lasts about 2 to 3 weeks depending on nail growth and daily activities.

Does gel polish dry right away?

Yes. Gel polish cures under a lamp, so it is dry before you leave the salon.

Can I add nail art?

Yes. French, chrome, cat eye, and simple designs can be added.

Is gel good for short nails?

Yes. Gel manicures look beautiful on short natural nails.

Do you remove old gel?

Yes. We can remove old gel polish professionally before your new manicure.
