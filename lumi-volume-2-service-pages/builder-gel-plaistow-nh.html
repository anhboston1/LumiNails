Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does Builder Gel last?

Most guests enjoy Builder Gel for about 3 to 4 weeks before needing a fill, depending on nail growth and lifestyle.

Can Builder Gel be filled?

Yes. Builder Gel can usually be filled, making it a great maintenance service.

Is Builder Gel better than acrylic?

It depends on your goal. Builder Gel is usually lighter and more natural-looking, while acrylic is often chosen for very long or dramatic extensions.

Does Builder Gel damage natural nails?

Professional application and removal help protect the natural nail. Avoid picking or peeling the product off.

Can I add nail art to Builder Gel?

Yes. Builder Gel pairs beautifully with French, chrome, cat eye, ombré, and custom nail art.
