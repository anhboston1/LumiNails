Frequently Asked Questions

Is acrylic stronger than Gel X?

Acrylic is often chosen for strong structure and dramatic length, while Gel X is often lighter and more flexible.

Can I get nail art on acrylic?

Yes. Acrylic works well with polish, French, chrome, ombré, and custom designs.

How often do acrylic nails need fills?

Many guests schedule fills every 2 to 3 weeks depending on growth.

Can acrylic be removed safely?

Yes. Professional removal is recommended.

Which is better, acrylic or Gel X?

It depends on your desired length, feel, and style. We can help you choose during your appointment.
