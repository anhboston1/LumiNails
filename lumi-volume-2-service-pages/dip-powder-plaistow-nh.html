Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does Dip Powder last?

Dip Powder often lasts about 2 to 4 weeks depending on nail growth and aftercare.

Is Dip Powder good for weak nails?

It can add strength, but guests with very weak nails may prefer Builder Gel for more structured support.

Can Dip Powder be filled?

Depending on the condition and style, some dip sets may be filled, but a fresh set is often recommended for the cleanest result.

Can I get French Dip?

Yes. French Dip is a classic, clean look and is available at Lumi Nails.

Should Dip Powder be professionally removed?

Yes. Professional removal helps prevent unnecessary damage to the natural nail.
