Frequently Asked Questions

How long does Gel X last?

Gel X usually lasts around 2 to 4 weeks depending on nail growth, aftercare, and lifestyle.

Is Gel X lighter than acrylic?

Yes. Many guests choose Gel X because it feels lighter and more flexible than traditional acrylic.

Can Gel X be removed safely?

Yes. Professional removal is recommended to protect your natural nails.

Can I get French or ombré with Gel X?

Yes. Gel X looks beautiful with French, ombré, chrome, cat eye, and custom designs.

Is Gel X good for vacation?

Yes. Gel X is a popular choice before travel because it gives instant length and a polished look.
