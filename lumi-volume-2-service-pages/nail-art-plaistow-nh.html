Frequently Asked Questions

Should I bring inspiration photos?

Yes. Inspiration photos help us understand your preferred style, color, and level of detail.

How much does nail art cost?

Pricing depends on complexity. Many designs range from $5 to $8 per finger.

Can I add nail art to Builder Gel?

Yes. Builder Gel is a great base for nail art.

Can I add nail art to Gel X?

Yes. Gel X works beautifully with French, chrome, ombré, and custom art.

Do detailed designs take longer?

Yes. Please book extra time for detailed or multi-finger designs.
