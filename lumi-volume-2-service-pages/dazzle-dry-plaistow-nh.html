Frequently Asked Questions

What is Dazzle Dry?

Dazzle Dry is a fast-drying nail polish system that gives beautiful shine without needing a gel lamp.

Is Dazzle Dry the same as gel?

No. Dazzle Dry is not gel polish.

How long does Dazzle Dry last?

Wear time varies by lifestyle, but many guests love it as a longer-lasting alternative to traditional polish.

Can I get Dazzle Dry on toes?

Yes. Dazzle Dry can be added to pedicures.

Who should choose Dazzle Dry?

It is great for guests who want a cleaner-feeling polish option and do not want gel.
