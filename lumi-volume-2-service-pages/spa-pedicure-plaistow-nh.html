Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between a classic and spa pedicure?

A spa pedicure includes more relaxation-focused steps such as scrubs, masks, massage, hot stones, or paraffin depending on the tier.

Can I choose my scent?

When available, yes. We offer scent options for selected pedicure services.

Can I add gel polish?

Yes. Gel polish can be added to most pedicure tiers.

Is a spa pedicure good for dry feet?

Yes. Scrubs, masks, massage, and paraffin can help the feet feel softer and refreshed.

Do I need an appointment?

Appointments are recommended, especially for longer spa pedicures.
