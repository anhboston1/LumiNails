Dazzle Dry Manicure in Plaistow, New Hampshire

Dazzle Dry is a fast-drying polish system for guests who prefer traditional-style removal and no UV curing. It offers a polished finish while helping you return to your day sooner.

Book Dazzle Dry ManicureAll Services

What to expect

  • Natural-nail shaping and cuticle care
  • Dazzle Dry system application
  • Fast air-drying without a UV lamp
  • Color selected from available Dazzle Dry shades

Who it is for

Guests who prioritize fast drying, vegan polish, no UV lamp and simpler at-home removal than gel.

Choosing your service

Choose Dazzle Dry when fast air drying is most important. Choose gel when longer wear and a cured glossy finish are your main priorities.

Questions

Dazzle Dry Manicure FAQs

Does Dazzle Dry use a UV lamp?

No. The system air dries and does not require UV curing.

How fast does Dazzle Dry dry?

The system is designed to dry quickly, though exact timing can vary with application and conditions.

Is Dazzle Dry the same as gel?

No. Dazzle Dry is a polish system; gel uses light curing and a different removal process.
