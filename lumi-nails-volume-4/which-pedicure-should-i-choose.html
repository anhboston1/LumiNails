Which Lumi Pedicure Should I Choose?

A simple four-level guide to Lumi’s pedicure menu, from maintenance to ultimate luxury.

Reviewed for Lumi Nails • Updated August 17, 2026

Lumi Essential Pedicure — $49

Choose Essential for straightforward maintenance: foot soak, nail and cuticle care, exfoliation, massage and polish. It is the practical choice when regular upkeep matters more than extended spa treatments.

Lumi Botanical Pedicure — $79

Choose Botanical when you want Lumi’s popular balance of treatment and relaxation. The service adds botanical elements, mask, pressure-point massage and warm comfort details.

Lumi Silk Signature Pedicure — $95

Choose Silk Signature for a fuller luxury ritual with hot-stone massage, silk milk treatment, paraffin and more time devoted to softness and relaxation.

Lumi Royal Ozone Pedicure — $125

Choose Royal Ozone for Lumi’s most elevated pedicure, featuring ozone steam therapy, premium herbal elements, advanced treatments and extended massage.

A quick decision rule

Book Essential for upkeep, Botanical for balanced indulgence, Silk Signature for deeper hot-stone relaxation, or Royal Ozone when the appointment itself is the main self-care experience.

Good to know: This guide provides general salon education, not medical advice. Nail condition and product wear vary by person.
