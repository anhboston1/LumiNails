Gel-X vs. Acrylic Nails: What Is the Difference?

Gel-X and acrylic can both create length, but the materials and application are different.

Reviewed for Lumi Nails • Updated August 17, 2026

How Gel-X works

Gel-X uses pre-shaped, full-cover soft-gel tips attached with a gel system and cured. The tips create length and shape across the entire nail. Aprés describes its Gel-X system as soft gel and soak-off.

How acrylic works

Traditional acrylic uses liquid monomer and powder polymer that combine and harden. A technician can sculpt it or apply it over tips. Acrylic is known for customization and rigidity, but it is a different product family from Gel-X.

Feel, maintenance and removal

Many guests choose Gel-X for a lighter-feeling, consistently shaped extension. Acrylic may appeal to people who prefer a sculpted, rigid system. Both require professional application and careful maintenance. Gel-X is designed as a soak-off soft-gel system; removal should still be performed patiently and never forced.

What Lumi Nails offers

Lumi Nails does not offer traditional acrylic services. We specialize in Gel-X, Hybrid/Builder Gel and dip powder. Choose Gel-X when added length is the priority, or compare Builder Gel when you want structure over your natural nail.

Good to know: This guide provides general salon education, not medical advice. Nail condition and product wear vary by person.
Common Questions

Frequently asked questions

Is Gel-X the same as acrylic?

No. Gel-X uses full-cover soft-gel tips and gel; acrylic uses a liquid-and-powder system.

Does Gel-X last as long as acrylic?

Wear varies. Aprés states properly applied Gel-X can last about three to four weeks, but growth, lifestyle, length and aftercare affect every set.

Can Gel-X be removed by peeling?

No. Peeling can damage layers of the natural nail. Book appropriate removal or maintenance.
