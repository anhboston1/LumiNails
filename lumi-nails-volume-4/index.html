About Lumi Nails

Thoughtful nail care, created for Plaistow.

Lumi Nails is a locally owned nail salon at Great Elm Plaza in Plaistow, New Hampshire. We created Lumi to give guests a calmer, more comfortable way to enjoy beautiful nails—combining attentive service, modern techniques and spa-inspired relaxation in a bright, welcoming space.

Feel beautiful, yet stay natural.

Our menu is designed for real life: polished manicures, restorative pedicures and modern enhancement systems with options for different lengths, styles and maintenance needs. Instead of traditional acrylic services, Lumi specializes in Gel-X, Hybrid Gel and dipping powder, along with natural-nail care and fast-drying Dazzle Dry manicures.

Whether you are visiting from Plaistow, Atkinson, Kingston, Hampstead, Newton or nearby Haverhill, our goal is simple: make booking easy, explain your options clearly and help you leave feeling refreshed and confident.

Our Promise

Our Service Guarantee

At Lumi Nails, we take pride in the quality of our work and want every guest to leave happy with their nails.

If you experience lifting, chipping, or other workmanship-related issues within 7 days of your service, please contact us as soon as possible. We will gladly repair the affected nail(s) at no additional charge.

If you're traveling or otherwise unable to return to our salon within the guarantee period, please let us know. Depending on the circumstances, we may offer a salon credit or reimburse the reasonable cost of repairing the affected nail(s) at another licensed nail salon.

Please notify us before having your nails repaired or removed by another salon, as doing so may limit our ability to determine the cause of the issue.

Refunds are generally not provided for completed services. Our goal is always to make things right in a fair and reasonable way.
