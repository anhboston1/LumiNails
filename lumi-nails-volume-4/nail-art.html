Nail Art in Plaistow, New Hampshire

Nail art turns your manicure or enhancement service into something personal. Lumi’s style ranges from soft, refined accents to seasonal details, French finishes and statement designs.

Pricing varies
Book Nail ArtAll Services

What to expect

  • Design discussion based on your inspiration
  • Placement and color planning
  • Art added to an eligible manicure or enhancement service
  • Finishing details for a cohesive look

Who it is for

Guests preparing for events, holidays or everyday self-expression, and anyone who wants a custom detail beyond a single color.

Choosing your service

Design complexity, number of accent nails and products used affect time and price. Add nail art when booking and bring inspiration photos when helpful.

Questions

Nail Art FAQs

Can I book nail art by itself?

Nail art is generally added to an eligible manicure, Gel-X, Hybrid Gel or dip service.

How much does nail art cost?

Pricing varies by complexity, time and number of nails. Ask for guidance when planning a detailed design.

Should I send an inspiration photo?

Yes. A clear reference helps the team understand the colors, detail and time your design may require.
