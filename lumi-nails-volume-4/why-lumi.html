Why Lumi

A Plaistow nail salon designed around comfort and clarity

Beautiful nails matter, but the experience matters too. Lumi combines modern services, thoughtful surroundings and clear guidance so your visit feels easier from beginning to end.

Book Your AppointmentCompare Services

Clean, airy salon

A bright, well-ventilated space designed for a comfortable appointment.

Clear service menu

Descriptions and service levels make it easier to choose what fits your needs.

Acrylic-free approach

Gel-X, Hybrid Gel and dip powder provide modern enhancement choices.

Spa pedicure options

Choose essential care, botanical relaxation, hot stones or Royal Ozone luxury.

Easy online booking

Browse availability and reserve online through Vagaro at your convenience.

Seven-day guarantee

Contact us about workmanship-related issues within seven days so we can help.

Modern services for different needs

Some guests want simple upkeep. Others need strength, added length, long wear or deeper relaxation. Lumi offers natural-nail manicures, gel, Dazzle Dry, dip powder, Hybrid Gel, Gel-X and four pedicure levels so you can choose based on your goal—not guess from a confusing menu.

Local and convenient

Lumi Nails is located at 37 Plaistow Rd, Unit #1 in Great Elm Plaza, with convenient parking and online booking. We welcome guests from Plaistow, Atkinson, Kingston, Hampstead, Newton, Haverhill and surrounding communities.
