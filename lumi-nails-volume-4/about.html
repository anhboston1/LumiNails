Our Story

About Lumi Nails in Plaistow, New Hampshire

Lumi Nails is a locally owned salon created to make nail care feel calmer, clearer and more personal—from easy online booking to the final polished result.

Book Your VisitWhy Guests Choose Lumi

Feel beautiful, yet stay natural.

We designed Lumi Nails for guests who want a bright, welcoming environment, attentive service and modern nail choices without an overwhelming experience. Our team takes time to understand the look, length, maintenance level and relaxation you want from your appointment.

Located at Great Elm Plaza, Lumi serves Plaistow and nearby communities including Atkinson, Kingston, Hampstead, Newton and Haverhill.

What we specialize in

Our menu includes natural-nail manicures, non-toxic gel manicures, Dazzle Dry, dipping powder, Gel-X extensions, Hybrid Gel and a range of spa pedicures.

We do not offer traditional acrylic services. Instead, we focus on Gel-X, Hybrid Gel and dipping powder as modern enhancement options with different strengths, lengths and finishes.
